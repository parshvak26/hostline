Reservations, answered.

Press the button and just talk. Our AI host will ask when you would like to come, how many of you there are, and your name — then check whether a table is really free.

Interrupt it. Change your mind. It keeps up.

·

Your booking, the transcript, and every decision about tables stay in this browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no database.

When the AI host is switched on, what you say is sent as text to an AI provider, and the reply is sent to a voice provider to be spoken. In Chrome, speech recognition is done by the browser itself, which sends audio to Google — that is a property of the browser, not of this page.

In simple mode nothing leaves your device at all. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. Read more.
